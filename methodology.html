MODEL CONTROL

Methodology

How GISit calculates an independent in-season dry-bean outlook and keeps USDA inputs, forecasts and final outcomes in their proper roles.

What the GISit estimate is

GISit v1 is an experimental, pinto-basis regional yield model. It uses a regularized equation trained on 92 published state-year pinto-yield outcomes and daily weather features: thermal onset, base-50°F growing degree days, precipitation, reference evapotranspiration and critical-stage climatic deficit.

What USDA data does—and does not—do

Current USDA yield forecasts are not predictors. Final USDA yield outcomes are used only as historical calibration and post-season validation targets. USDA planted and expected-harvested acres are separate, date-correct production inputs. Every acreage update retains its issue date, status and prior value.

Selected-date discipline

The model uses weather available through the selected date. Later days are completed with the 2000–2025 day-of-year median, excluding a held season during backtesting. April and early-June estimates are withheld because their hindcast does not beat the historical-median baseline; the 2026 release gate first passes at the June 15 checkpoint.

Validation and uncertainty

Each calendar year is held out from model training and from its completion climatology. The terminal hindcast mean absolute error is 167 lb/ac versus 179 lb/ac for the state historical-median baseline. The displayed interval is the empirical 80th-percentile absolute-error band at the latest completed validation checkpoint; it is not a probability invented by the interface.

Crop-health evidence

A crop-health outlook is released only when the yield gate passes and two separate Crop-CASMA evidence families are present: NASA SMAP L4 root-zone moisture anomaly and the direction of change in encoded NDVI. Weekly neighborhood samples retain requested and actual source-valid dates. These generalized neighborhoods are not field measurements.

Phenology

The planting-onset proxy is the first day local cumulative GDD reaches the historical Nebraska Panhandle thermal total associated with a sourced June 7 planting reference. Stage boundaries are scaled to the sourced 1,550–1,700 GDD maturity range. A reported local planting date or official crop-progress observation should override this weaker proxy when available.

Maps and geography

County boundaries are reference only. Yield circles are analytical neighborhoods, and SMAP/NDVI layers are landscape evidence. None is represented as planted-acre geography. A USDA Cropland Data Layer class-42 mask remains required before field-like or crop-footprint display.

Known limitations

The current model does not observe irrigation application, cultivar, disease, hail, soil constraints or management. It uses pinto outcomes as the training target and therefore is not a universal dry-bean class model. Kansas output is withheld because its published pinto-yield series ends in 2015.

Classification

Observed is a directly obtained source record or measurement. Derived, Modeled, Estimated, Assumed, and Unknown retain the meanings in the master execution contract. Verified is reserved for work status.

Publication workflow

Source → ingest → preserve checksum and dates → build → backtest → apply release gate → render → verify exact deployed build → publish. A source update does not silently rewrite the history shown for an earlier date.
