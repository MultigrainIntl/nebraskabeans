EDITORIAL CONTROL

Methodology

How NebraskaBeans separates evidence, interpretation and commercial information.

Source hierarchy

Primary government statistics and official university extension material are preferred for production, crop and weather facts. Important figures retain source, units, observation period and publication context.

Classification

Verified means supported by direct source evidence. Estimated means a clearly labeled inference. Unknown means evidence is insufficient. Pending means an expected official release has not yet been incorporated.

No fabricated completeness

Unavailable crop progress, production, price, freight, processor or trade figures are not filled with plausible values. Empty states are intentional when the source does not support a current number.

Maps and visualization

The V1 Nebraska map is geographic context, not a measured county production surface. A future production layer must preserve the underlying public dataset, vintage and methodology.

Commercial separation

Public agricultural intelligence is structurally separate from private buyer, seller, grower, processor, logistics and GIS intelligence. No private commercial records are embedded in browser-delivered data.

Future publication workflow

Source → Ingest → Curate → Draft → Cite → Verify → Approve → Publish. Automated scraping or AI drafting does not authorize automatic publication.
