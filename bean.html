Nebraska significance

Nebraska Department of Agriculture and UNL CropWatch identify Nebraska as a leading U.S. dry bean state, with production concentrated in western Nebraska. Class-specific rankings and acreage are published only when authoritative data are available and not withheld.

Quality & specifications

Commercial contracts should specify grade, moisture, defects, foreign material, sizing where relevant, packaging, inspection requirements and crop year. NebraskaBeans does not publish unverified tolerances; buyers should reference the current USDA bean inspection standards and agreed contract specifications.

Packaging & logistics

Commercial routing can accommodate bulk, totes and bagged product, subject to seller capability and current availability. Domestic and export moves may use truck, rail/intermodal and ocean containers. Freight and transit times require a current quote.
