MODEL TRANSPARENCY

What this recovery build will and will not claim

VERIFIED

Repository/staging baseline, county geometry as reference, official published agricultural statistics and direct observations where actually obtained.

MODELED

Reanalysis, SMAP data assimilation, HLS vegetation indices, gridded forecasts and other external models remain labeled modeled.

ESTIMATED

GISit-derived crop stage, water status, condition, yield, confidence and commercial interpretation only after required evidence is connected and validated.

ASSUMED

Necessary model assumptions must be explicit, sourced where possible and sensitivity-tested. The former fixed 2,300 lb/ac baseline and June 1 planting anchor are not accepted as final architecture.

Recovery rule: a weather signal is not automatically crop stress; a county is not a crop polygon; a satellite image is not a yield estimate; a deployed feature is not verified until its rendered behavior and evidence chain pass testing.
