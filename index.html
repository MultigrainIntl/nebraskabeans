What is still not claimed

No current county acreage, private field boundaries, processor inventory, yield loss or production shortfall is inferred without supporting evidence. USDA county dry-bean estimates are discontinued.

1 · Dry-Bean Stage + Water Tool

UNL dry-bean irrigation guidance provides verified late-season anchors: R5 ≈ 35 days to maturity and 7.0 inches water remaining; R6 ≈ 25 days / 4.2 inches; R7 ≈ 15 days / 2.0 inches; R8 = maturity. Earlier stage is deliberately broader unless field evidence is supplied.

2 · Station vs Model Verification

GISit queries NOAA GHCN-Daily around the selected point and compares direct observed precipitation with the modeled 30-day signal.

Waiting for station evidence.

Nebraska Mesonet is an additional Nebraska-specific ground network with 56 stations in 45 counties measuring hourly weather, soil temperature and soil moisture. Its data/service interface should be connected server-side when an approved machine-readable endpoint is confirmed.

3 · Production + Satellite Tool

Verified frame: Nebraska dry edible bean production is concentrated in western Nebraska. The Panhandle and North Platte River Valley are the primary decision geography.
