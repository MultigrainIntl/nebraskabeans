MODEL TRANSPARENCY

What is measured, modeled and assumed

VERIFIED

U.S. Census county geometry is retained as the visible reference boundary. Historical USDA and extension evidence is used to locate dry-bean production regions in Nebraska, Colorado, Wyoming and Kansas. Current government weather-station observations are displayed as direct evidence where available.

MODELED

Open-Meteo historical hourly soil moisture at 7–28 cm and 28–100 cm is converted to daily means and combined with daily precipitation, ET₀ and temperature at a denser set of source points. The visible color field is an IDW interpolation between those source points.

ESTIMATED

Root-zone moisture, crop stage, crop condition, yield adjustment and yield range are GISit calculations for the selected date. Rounded crop-region envelopes are derived analytical regions, not field boundaries.

ASSUMED

Root-zone composite = 35% of 7–28 cm plus 65% of 28–100 cm. Until authoritative regional yield history and planting dates are connected, the Nebraska yield panel also uses a 2,300 lb/ac working baseline and June 1 planting anchor.

Important: This is a decision-support estimate, not an official USDA forecast. County lines are retained for reference; county fills are not used as crop-condition areas. Direct station observations remain distinct from gridded/interpolated model evidence.
