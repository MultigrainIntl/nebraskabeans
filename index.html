MODEL TRANSPARENCY

What this staging prototype will and will not claim

OBSERVED

A directly obtained source record or measurement. Publication status such as estimate or forecast remains visible.

DERIVED / MODELED

Deterministic transformations remain separate from reanalysis, remote sensing, forecasts, and other model outputs.

ESTIMATED

GISit-derived stage, water status, condition, yield, confidence, and interpretation require connected evidence and validation.

ASSUMED / UNKNOWN

Assumptions are explicit and sensitivity-tested; insufficient evidence carries no claimed value.

Recovery rule: a USDA forecast is an observed publication record but remains a forecast; weather is not automatically crop stress; a county is not a crop polygon; a satellite image is not a yield estimate; deployment is not verification.
