MODEL TRANSPARENCY

What the experimental GISit v1 model does

CALIBRATION

92 published state-year pinto-yield outcomes from 2000–2025 train a regularized weather/GDD equation. Current USDA yield forecasts are excluded.

IN-SEASON COMPLETION

Actual modeled weather is used through the selected date. Unobserved days use the 2000–2025 day-of-year median, preventing future-season leakage.

VALIDATION + RANGE

Leave-one-calendar-year-out testing must beat a state historical-median baseline. The displayed range is the empirical 80th-percentile absolute-error band.

PRODUCTION

Production is withheld until crop-area weights, compatible market-class acreage and yield, and propagated uncertainty pass validation. USDA acreage revisions remain visible.

Known limits: yield markers are experimental analytical points; current irrigation, cultivar, disease, hail and management are not observed; satellite neighborhoods are not yet CDL-masked; the pinto calibration is not a universal dry-bean class model. Kansas yield is withheld because its target history ends in 2015.
