MODEL TRANSPARENCY

How the yield figure is built

WHAT IS MEASURED

Canopy greenness from satellite. Dry beans are sampled on Cropland Data Layer bean pixels; chickpeas, lentils and peas are sampled at one point per county, which is a far weaker measurement and is labelled as such. Temperature and rainfall come from weather stations. Solar radiation is not measured — it is estimated from the daily temperature range.

WHY NO YIELD IS SHOWN

A radiation-use-efficiency model runs behind this site, but its output is not published. An independent review found the step converting satellite greenness to intercepted light is uncalibrated, and that the efficiency and harvest-index constants are not sourced per market class. Until those are established against real harvest data, a number here would imply precision the evidence does not carry.

PLANTING DATE IS MOSTLY THE CALENDAR

The published normal date acts as a floor that weather can only push later, never earlier, and there is no field-workability test in the code despite an earlier claim here that there was. In 2026 nearly every class came out on its normal date in all seven regions. Treat planting date as an assumption.

EVERY CLASS, EVERY REGION

Eighteen market classes across seven growing regions, each with its own base temperature, heat threshold and maturity requirement. Rebuilt every morning from sources that need no key and no account.

What this is not. This is not a validated forecast. The level is defensible; the season-to-season ranking is not — tested against USDA over 2016–2023 it did not rank one season against another correctly, and that error cannot be separated from the scorecard’s: USDA revised Nebraska’s 2026 planted acres by 21% mid-season, reports yield per harvested acre so abandoned fields vanish from it, and stopped publishing county yields in 2008. Water stress is read at 1 km, so a pixel mixes a crop field with the ground around it, biasing every crop toward looking more stressed than it is. Light-use efficiency and harvest index are published values per crop group, not fitted to these fields. Irrigation scheduling, cultivar, disease, hail and management are not observed.
