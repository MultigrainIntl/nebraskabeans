Geography Rule

USDA NASS stopped publishing county-level dry edible bean estimates beginning with the 2019 crop year. This tool therefore uses verified Nebraska production references and research locations without inventing current county acreage or intensity.

1 · Nebraska Production Geography

Verified regional frame: Nebraska dry edible bean production is concentrated in western Nebraska. UNL reporting identifies the Nebraska Panhandle and North Platte River Valley as active dry-bean production geography.

2 · Nebraska Dry-Bean Crop Timing

Enter the best-known field planting or emergence date. The result is an ESTIMATE, not a field-observed growth stage.

Nebraska evidence: planting is underway by late May in NASS/UNL reporting; western Nebraska examples include May 28 and June 7 planting and September harvest, while a 2023 Grant trial planted June 16 and harvested September 28. Generic dry-bean development-day bands are used only as a regional calibration aid, never as verified field stage.

3 · NOAA Station vs Model

For the selected Nebraska point, the tool queries NOAA NCEI GHCN-Daily observations in a surrounding box and compares observed precipitation with the model-derived 30-day precipitation signal.

Select a location above. Direct station evidence will appear here when NOAA returns a nearby reporting station.
