MODEL TRANSPARENCY

What is measured, modeled and assumed

VERIFIED

2026 Nebraska intended area: 101,000 acres. U.S. Census county geometry is used for the regional polygons.

MODELED

Open-Meteo historical daily soil moisture at 7–28 cm and 28–100 cm, precipitation, ET₀ and temperature are sampled at multiple points inside each polygon.

ESTIMATED

Polygon-average root-zone moisture, crop stage, crop condition, yield adjustment and yield range are GISit calculations from the evidence available at the selected date.

ASSUMED

Root-zone composite = 35% of 7–28 cm plus 65% of 28–100 cm. Until authoritative regional yield history and planting dates are connected, the model also uses a 2,300 lb/ac baseline and June 1 planting anchor.

Important: This is a decision-support estimate, not an official USDA forecast. County boundaries are analytical containers, not claims about bean acreage or field boundaries.
